# Model credits and licenses

The following CC BY 4.0 models are bundled with Pocket Clash. Links open their original creator or source page. Local copies of the license notes are next to each GLB under `public/models/`.

- **Doraemon** by [satriek](https://sketchfab.com/satriek), [original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/doraemon-b11372b0c29a4ef4b9691180d05870ec), CC BY 4.0. Normalized, smoothed and rigged in Blender for this game.
- **Teletubby character model** by **@falterize**, [source repository](https://github.com/falterize/katsa-engine/tree/main/assets/models/characters), CC BY 4.0. Recolored and fitted with an antenna and a screen for Po.
- **Naruto Rigged (HatchXR)** by [nitwit.friends](https://sketchfab.com/nitwit.friends), [original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/naruto-rigged-hatchxr-b03f67238a654141b61720d214ef1b44), CC BY 4.0. The original skeleton and animation clips are retained and connected to the game's combat moves.

CC BY 4.0 requires attribution, a link to the license, and an indication of changes. The in-game credits panel links the [full license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
