Sasuke Uchiha and Perfect Susanoo
Created and supplied by player dx0018 for this game.

Source uploads:
- sasuke-uchiha-detailed.obj.zip
- samurai-archer-detailed.obj.zip
Both contain sculptor-v2.obj, sculptor-v2.mtl, and Image_0.png.

Changes: normalized proportions (Sasuke 3.1 units, Susanoo 6.2 units), added
articulated skeletons and skin weights, smoothed normals, adjusted materials,
compressed textures to 1024x1024 JPEG, exported browser-ready GLBs, and added
procedural combat poses. The bow is rigidly bound with authored tip sockets;
the baked arrow/resting string are replaced by synchronized game effects. Converter: scripts/assets/import-sasuke.py.

The partial skeletal Susanoo (ribs, skull, articulated arms, chakra blade)
is original game geometry, defined in src/render/susanoo-skeleton.js.
No third-party open-content license is asserted for the user-supplied models.
