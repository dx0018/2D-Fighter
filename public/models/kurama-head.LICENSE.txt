Kurama Fox Head (extracted GLB model)

Original model: Fox by PixelMannen, 2014, CC0 1.0 Universal.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/fox-and-shiba

Rigging and animation: tomkranis, 2014, CC BY 4.0.
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-fox-by-pixelmannen-animated-371dea88d7e04a76af5763f2a36866bc

glTF conversion: @AsoboStudio and @scurest, 2017, CC BY 4.0.
Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/Fox

Changes for this game: extracted the vertices assigned to the original head
joint, removed the rest of the fox and its rig, enlarged and centered the head,
and added glowing eye details and a warm chakra emissive material. The nine
chakra tails remain a separate in-game effect.
